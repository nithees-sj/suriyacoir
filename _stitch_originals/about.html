THE FOUNDER'S NOTE
"Sustainability isn't a feature; it's the foundation of the future. We treat the Earth's fibers with the reverence they deserve."

With three decades in the industry, Mr. Veda saw the potential of coconut husks not as waste, but as a masterpiece of natural engineering. His journey began in a small shed with a single mill, driven by the belief that local materials could command global prestige.

Today, he leads a team of 400 artisans and engineers, blending traditional hand-sorting techniques with state-of-the-art hydraulic extraction.
